Lecture 6

Flows, symmetries and the symplectic form

What this lecture does

Lecture 5 made the observables an algebra: every observable changes along a motion at the rate of its bracket with HHH. This lecture asks what flow an observable generates, and what that flow preserves. Every observable fff generates a flow, as HHH generates the motion; momentum generates translations, angular momentum rotations. The flows preserve the bracket, and Jacobi is exactly the reason; brackets of observables become brackets of their flows; and a quantity is conserved exactly when HHH is invariant under its flow — Noether’s theorem, now in both directions. Behind the bracket stands one bilinear form ω\sympω, the sum of oriented areas, and the flows preserve it: their Jacobian matrices are symplectic. For quadratic Hamiltonians this is the Lie algebra sp(2n)\splie(2n)sp(2n). With this, the second answer is almost complete.
